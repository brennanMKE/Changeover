How to read this
----------------
"not captured" is absence of evidence, never disagreement: those discs
were archived before Tools/menudump existed and get their menus the next
time they pass through the drive.

1 disc has an answer from both sources: 1 agree, 0 disagree.
No disagreement so far. One-sided agreement on a handful of discs is not
evidence of a trustworthy rule — §11.4 stays "no" until the compared
column is large and still clean.

Play All discs (tv-season-playall): the heuristic names a title and then
refuses it. A menu that points at the same title is agreeing about the
title, not about ripping it.
