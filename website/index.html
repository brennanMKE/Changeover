Put a DVD in.
Get it in Plex.

Changeover turns a shelf of discs into a Plex library. Put one in and it works out what the film is, rips it, files it the way Plex expects, and ejects the disc so you can load the next one.

Download for macOS Version 0.1.0 · Apple silicon · macOS 26.2 or later

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Free and open source.

Smart import

It works out what the disc is, and shows you how

A DVD barely tells you what it holds. The label is often the title with the spaces taken out — sometimes with letters missing. The menus are pictures, not text. There are a dozen titles on the disc and only one is the film.

Doing this by hand means reading the label, guessing, searching, comparing runtimes, and picking a title number — for every disc. Changeover does it in about fifteen seconds, and shows its work while it happens: 

▶ Reading the disc's menus — 21 pages to look at
  · vtsm-01-lu1-pgc1: PLAY MOVIE · SCENE SELECTION · SPECIAL FEATURES
  · vtsm-01-lu1-pgc28: SCENES · 1-4 5-8 9-12 · MAIN MENU
▶ Identifying the disc — its label reads "BASKBALL"
  · "Baskball" found nothing on TMDB — looking at the disc's menus
  · asking this Mac's on-device model to read 34 lines of menu text
  · the model reads it as "BASEketball"
▶ Matching against TMDB — the disc's feature runs 103 min
  · BASEketball — 103 min · Trey Parker, Matt Stone
  · Baseketball Legends — 48 min
✓ Chose BASEketball — its title matches and its runtime fits the disc

Vision reads the words off each menu screen, because a DVD stores its menus as pictures. Apple's on-device model turns a mangled label into a real title. Then runtime and billed cast decide between films of the same name — and when the evidence is thin, it says so and lets you choose rather than guessing on your behalf.

And the rest of it

Picks the right part

A DVD holds trailers, menus, and sometimes the same film twice — once widescreen, once cropped for an old television. Changeover finds the feature, prefers the widescreen transfer, and refuses to encode a two-minute trailer just because the disc called it the main title.

Files it the way Plex wants

Movies/Title (Year) {tmdb-ID}/Title (Year).mp4, every time, with the database id that lets Plex tell two films of the same name apart. Different cuts of one film sit side by side as editions.

Runs without you

It ejects each disc when it's done, so the open tray is the signal to load the next one. It keeps working on a Mac whose screen has locked — including discs put in after the lock, which macOS would otherwise spit straight back out.

What you need

  • A Mac running macOS 26.2 or later, with a USB DVD drive.
  • HandBrake's command-line tool: brew install handbrake
  • A free TMDB API key, for looking films up.
  • Somewhere to put the files — a Plex library folder, local or on a NAS.

Changeover only reads discs you own, and only the ones you put in the drive.
