## Resolution notes

> 🟢 Resolved and proven on joe, 2026-09-25 — a process that registers a `DARegisterDiskEjectApprovalCallback` refuses `loginwindow`'s eject, the disc stays in the drive, and `HandBrakeCLI --input /dev/rdisk4` reads it with no mount at all. The disc's own label is recovered from libdvdnav's `DVD Title:` line during the scan.

## Description

`loginwindow` installs mount- and unmount-approval callbacks while the screen is locked, so a disc inserted into a locked Mac never mounts and is ejected again moments later. There was no `/Volumes/NAME` to hand to HandBrake and no volume name to identify the film from — the app simply could not work a disc on a headless or locked machine, which is the normal state of a Plex host.

Disabling the screen lock was considered and **rejected**: it is a real security regression users will not accept, and it does not help a genuinely headless Mac.

The solution works with the lock in place, in three parts:

1. **Hold the disc.** Eject is a vote, not a command — `diskarbitrationd` polls approval callbacks, and one dissenter abandons the eject. The app registers a callback matched to a single BSD name and returns a dissenter, releasing it on disc removal, at job end, on quit, and immediately before its own eject.
2. **Read the raw device.** `RawDiscSource.devicePath(bsdName:)` yields `/dev/rdiskN`, which HandBrake reads directly with no mount.
3. **Recover the label.** libdvdnav prints the disc's label during the scan; `RawDiscSource.label(fromScanOutput:)` parses it and rejects placeholders.

`DiscInsertion` gained `isMounted`, and admits an unmounted disc on media kind alone (`IODVDMedia` / `IOBDMedia`). Every guard that used to compare whole `DiscInsertion` values now matches on `insertionID`, so one physical insertion stays the same insertion as the app learns more about it.

## Notes

Filed retrospectively on 2026-09-25. Its code comments cited **0073**, an unrelated filed ticket (the Changeover Remote app); renumbered to **0152**.

Because the DiskArbitration registration cannot be unit-tested without a real drive, every decision it makes is pushed into `DiscHoldPolicy` (`shouldHold(reason:heldFor:userAskedToEject:appIsQuitting:)`, 20-minute idle limit) where it is pinned. Covered by `HeadlessDiscTests`, `UnmountedDiscTests`, `DiscIdentityStabilityTests` and `DiscIdentityMatchingTests`.

The reasoning failures along the way — including recommending the screen lock be disabled, and inventing a `DisableScreenLockDiskPolicy` preference out of `strings` output — are recorded in `docs/lessons-from-the-locked-screen.md`.
