# 0020 — Phase 2: rip only the chosen titles and tracks instead of the whole disc

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | open |

| **Module** | DiscScanner / RipController / MetadataEntryView |
| **Platform** | macOS |
| **First seen** | 2026-09-11 |

## Description

Changeover rips every title on every disc and then guesses which one is the movie
by picking the largest `.mkv` on disk. Phase 2 replaces that with a real disc scan,
a structured title list, an identified main feature and explicit audio/subtitle
language selection, so a disc with fourteen titles rips one — and so a disc that is
not a movie says so instead of being filed as one.

## Long Description

This is the umbrella ticket for **Phase 2 — Choose what gets ripped** in `Roadmap.md`
(lines 94–129). It ships no code itself; the children below do.

Today `RipController.rip(makemkvconPath:outputDir:log:)` passes the literal string
`"all"` to `makemkvcon` (`Changeover/RipController.swift:19-24`) and then calls
`largestMKV(in:)` (`Changeover/RipController.swift:66-77`) to decide which of the
resulting files is the movie. Every consequence of that flows from the same root:
bonus features, trailers, FBI warnings and alternate angles all get ripped and
encoded-adjacent work is done on a file chosen by a size comparison over a shared
working directory. `mac_plex_dvd_workflow.md` §3.3 documents what a human actually
does in MakeMKV.app instead — select the largest title, keep English and Spanish
audio, uncheck alternate angles and bonus features — and Phase 2 is that manual
step, automated.

The phase had one genuine research risk, carved out as [#0021](0021.md), and **it
has been retired.** A disc-selection experiment on 2026-09-11 scanned eight DVDs and
checked 15 fixtures into `ChangeoverTests/Fixtures/makemkvcon/`. `DiscSelection.md`
is the authoritative writeup and everything in this block now defers to it.

The experiment shrank the phase and moved the difficulty. Five things changed:

1. **Main-feature selection collapsed to one comparison.** The movie is the only
   title ≥ 45 minutes — 8 of 9 discs. No scoring engine, no weights, **and no
   LLM**: the problem has one numeric feature and a threshold, which a comparison
   resolves exactly and a local model would resolve non-deterministically.
2. **…but the rule needs a guard.** The ninth disc, a Brooklyn Nine-Nine season,
   satisfies it and is wrong: its one long title is a "Play All" of eight episodes,
   and it is also the largest and the most-chaptered. Without [#0025](0025.md)'s
   Play All guard the app would file a season of television in the Plex Movies
   library, silently.
3. **`usage.txt` is wrong about MakeMKV's own output.** It documents
   `TINFO:id,code,value` and `TCOUT:count`; real output is
   `TINFO:title,id,code,value` and `TCOUNT:`. Do not cite it for line shapes —
   cite the fixtures.
4. **Title indices are not stable across `--minlength`, and neither are the output
   filenames.** `hanna-min0.txt` and `hanna-mindefault.txt` each contain a title 1
   named `D1_t01.mkv`; one is 13 seconds, the other is the whole movie.
   [#0028](0028.md) now verifies rips by duration.
5. **Track selection turned out to be the hard half.** Missing language tags,
   duplicate tracks and commentary detection are real problems with fixture
   evidence; [#0027](0027.md) carries them.

The remaining unknowns are named in [#0021](0021.md) and are honest gaps, not
footnotes: no captured disc has two feature-length titles, and no Disney/Pixar disc
has been scanned.

The models introduced in [#0022](0022.md) are deliberately designed to move into
the `ChangeoverProtocol` Swift package in Phase 4 ([#0060](0060.md)) without edits:
value types, `Codable`, `Sendable`, no AppKit or SwiftUI import, and no reliance on
this target's `SWIFT_DEFAULT_ACTOR_ISOLATION = MainActor` setting, which a Swift
package will not inherit.

## Demo

From `Roadmap.md` lines 99–106:

> Insert a disc that has bonus features. The app lists every title with its
> duration, chapter count and size, with the main feature already selected by the
> length heuristic. Show the audio track list — pick English and Spanish, drop the
> rest. Start. Point out that it is ripping one title instead of fourteen, and show
> the elapsed time against a full-disc rip of the same movie.

The experiment improved the demo rather than changing it. Two amendments worth
making, because both are more convincing than the original:

- **The app does not present a list to choose from — it presents an answer.** Use
  Hanna or Hornets' Nest, where the feature is title 3 or title 9, so "it picked the
  right one out of sixteen, and it was not the first" is visible. Expand the table
  to show what it rejected.
- **Then put in the Brooklyn Nine-Nine disc**, which has one 2:53:18 title with 33
  chapters at 6.9 GB, and show the app refusing to call it a movie. That is a better
  demo than the happy path: it is the moment the tool demonstrates it knows the
  difference between confidence and correctness.

## Expected behavior

Inserting a disc scans it once at an explicit `--minlength`, identifies the main
feature without asking, shows it as a confirmation row with the full title list one
click away, offers the audio and subtitle languages that title actually carries, and
rips exactly that title with exactly those languages. Extras are opt-in and land
outside the Plex roots. A disc that does not identify itself — no long title, two
long titles, or a TV "Play All" — says so instead of guessing.

## Actual behavior

Inserting a disc opens a TMDB search window
(`Changeover/AppDelegate.swift:60-65`, `Changeover/DVDMonitor.swift:15-27`). There
is no disc scan anywhere in the codebase, no title list, no track selection, and
`Start Ripping` (`Changeover/MetadataEntryView.swift:126-130`) launches a full-disc
rip.

## Exit criteria

From `Roadmap.md` lines 122–128:

- Title list matches what MakeMKV.app shows for the same disc.
- A disc with bonus features rips only the chosen title.
- Selected audio languages are present in the output and unselected ones are not.
- Scan failure (expired key, unreadable disc) is reported as itself, not as an
  empty title list.

Plus one added during breakdown:

- `largestMKV(in:)` is gone. The file handed to HandBrake is named by the scan, not
  discovered by a directory listing.

Plus five added after the disc-selection experiment — each one is a thing the
fixtures showed would otherwise ship broken:

- **The rip is verified by duration, not by filename.** `D1_t01.mkv` is a valid
  output name for two different titles on the same disc ([#0028](0028.md)).
- **One explicit `--minlength` is passed to both the scan and the rip**, and no
  title index is ever carried across two scans.
- **A scan that exits 0 is a success**, even carrying `MSG:2003` or `MSG:4004`;
  read errors surface as a warning, not a failure ([#0024](0024.md)).
- **A TV season disc is not filed as a movie.** `brooklyn-nine-nine-min0.txt` must
  produce a refusal, not a 6.9 GB entry in the Plex Movies library
  ([#0025](0025.md)).
- **Extras cannot reach `Movies/`.** The destination is a type, not a path string
  ([#0031](0031.md)).

## Notes

**Verification.** The phase splits cleanly into two halves. Everything that turns
bytes into models — the parser ([#0023](0023.md)), the models
([#0022](0022.md)), the heuristic and its Play All guard ([#0025](0025.md)) — is
pure and belongs in real `ChangeoverTests` unit tests running under
`xcodebuild -project Changeover.xcodeproj -scheme Changeover -destination 'platform=macOS' test`.
Everything that touches `makemkvcon` or `HandBrakeCLI` needs a physical disc in the
mini's drive and must say so plainly in its `## Verification`, per the caveat in
`issues/Issues.md`. Note that `ChangeoverTests/ChangeoverTests.swift` currently
contains only the Xcode stub `example()` test — Phase 2 is where this target starts
earning its place.

**The fixtures moved the line between those halves considerably.**
`ChangeoverTests/Fixtures/makemkvcon/` now holds 15 captures from eight discs, and
they are real test input, not documentation. The strongest automated verification in
the whole phase is [#0025](0025.md)'s Play All guard: a pure function over a list of
integers, nine fixtures that separate with an 80-point margin, and a failure mode
that is catastrophic and silent. Write that test.

What the fixtures still cannot verify, and the phase must say so rather than imply
otherwise: no disc with two feature-length titles, no Disney/Pixar disc, no PAL
disc, and exactly one failure capture (`keyexpired-v1.18.3-exit253.txt`) — so the
`.noDisc` branch of [#0024](0024.md) is written from reasoning, not evidence.

**Dependency on Phase 1.** [#0001](0001.md) extracts an `@Observable JobController`
out of `MetadataEntryView` and gives each job its own working subdirectory. Disc
scan results are job state, not view state, so [#0024](0024.md), [#0026](0026.md)
and [#0027](0027.md) all want that controller to exist first. The parser, models and
heuristic tickets have no such dependency and can start immediately.

**Swift conventions** (`Plan.md`, "Swift conventions"): `@Observable`, never
`ObservableObject`; view models owned with `@State`; `@Bindable` for bindings passed
into subviews; `SWIFT_DEFAULT_ACTOR_ISOLATION = MainActor`
(`Changeover.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj:416,445`) so `nonisolated` is written only
where a type genuinely must leave the main actor — as `RipController` and
`EncodeController` already do.
