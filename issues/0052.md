# 0052 — A disc removed while its job is running is never reported to the job, so the failure is blamed on the disc or on a timeout

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | open |
| **Phase** | 3 — Unattended multi-disc |
| **Module** | JobController / DVDPipeline / DiscReliabilityLog |
| **Platform** | macOS |
| **First seen** | 2026-09-15 |

## Description

Found by the Phase 3 review. When the disc leaves the drive mid-job — the drive's own eject button, a tray bump, a USB hiccup — `DVDMonitor.onDVDRemoved` fires and `AppDelegate` calls `jobs.removeDisc()` (`Changeover/AppDelegate.swift:113-115`). That method clears `insertedDisc`, `scanState` and every selection (`Changeover/JobController.swift:773-783`) and never looks at `current`. The running `DVDPipeline` learns nothing. What happens next depends on the drive:

- If `HandBrakeCLI`'s reads fail, the job ends `failed` with a disc-shaped reason (`.discUnreadable` or `.toolExited`), the notification says the disc could not be read, and `DiscReliabilityLog` writes a `failed` record against that disc's identity — a human act recorded as a bad disc.
- If the reads block, HandBrake goes silent and the job sits in "Encoding" until the 30-minute inactivity watchdog (#0009) fails it as `.unknown("…produced no output for 30 minutes…")`.

In neither case does any log line say the disc was removed. The only trace is that the metadata window went blank. The `FallbackPolicy.discStillPresent` closure (`Changeover/DVDPipeline.swift:506-510`) does the right thing — no MakeMKV attempt on a disc that is gone — but that is the only place the pipeline notices.

#0045's plan refresh specified this case ("When `onDVDRemoved` fires while `isRunning`, log a `⚠︎ Disc removed while a job was running` milestone and let HandBrake fail on its own"), and the #0040 orchestrator decided against a new `FailureReason.discRemoved` so older decoders keep working. The re-scope of #0045 to the manual Eject then dropped it, and nothing else picked it up. #0046's manual check 3 ("pull the disc mid-encode, then press Cancel, record how long HandBrakeCLI takes to exit") is about to exercise exactly this on joe with no code behind it.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Start a job on a disc.
2. Press the drive's eject button, or otherwise remove the disc, a minute in.
3. Read the job's log and, when it ends, its failure headline, the notification and `~/Library/Logs/Changeover/disc-reliability.jsonl`.

## Expected behavior

The job's own log carries a `⚠︎ Disc removed while the job was running` milestone at the moment of removal. The failure headline and notification say the disc was removed, not that it was unreadable or that HandBrake timed out. The reliability record is marked so the disc is not counted as unreadable. The job ends promptly rather than after 30 minutes of silence.

## Actual behavior

Nothing is logged. The job ends as a disc read failure or a 30-minute timeout, the notification blames the disc or the tool, and the reliability log records a disc failure.

## Plan

No new `FailureReason` case (the #0040 decision stands; `JobOutcome` is wire format). The fact is carried beside the outcome instead.

- **Notice it.** In `JobController.removeDisc()`, when `current` exists (under option A the job's disc is the only disc), append `⚠︎ Disc removed while the job was running` to `current.log` — the job's log, not `controllerLog` — and set a new `Job.discRemovedDuringJob = true` (host-only; not on `JobSnapshot` unless #0060 wants it).
- **Present it.** `FailurePresenter.message(for:)` gains an optional "disc removed" context, or `JobController.finish` / `JobNotifier` prepend it: headline "The disc was removed during the job", body unchanged. `JobPresentation.make(for:)` shows the same in the history row's detail, so #0048's view and the notification agree.
- **Record it.** Give `JobContext` a `discRemoved: @MainActor () -> Bool` closure (the shape `discStillPresent` already uses) and have `DVDPipeline` write `decision: "discRemoved"` into the `DiscReliabilityLog.Record` when it is true. `decision` is a `String?`, so this is additive and no decoder breaks; reliability analysis filters it out the way it already must filter `"cancelled"` (#0046).
- **End it promptly — user's call, two options.** (a) Log and present only; let HandBrake fail on its own, bounded by the 30-minute watchdog in the blocked-read case. (b) On removal during `.encoding`/`.fallback`/`.extras`, also call `task?.cancel()`, so the process is stopped through #0046's SIGTERM→SIGKILL path and the job ends within seconds. The cost of (b) is that the wire outcome is then `.cancelled` and the history row reads "Cancelled" unless the presentation reads `discRemovedDuringJob` first. The review recommends (b) with the presentation override: an unattended run should not sit on a pulled disc for half an hour.
- **Tests** (`JobControllerTests`, gated runner, no disc): `removeDisc()` while a job runs appends the milestone to that job's log and to nothing else, and sets the flag; with no job running it logs nothing into any job; the notification text and history detail for a flagged failure; the reliability record carries `decision: "discRemoved"` when the closure returns true (pipeline test against the stub `HandBrakeCLI`, redirecting `DiscReliabilityLog` to a temp file as the fallback tests do). If option (b): the job ends within the test's grace and the next `start` is accepted.
- **Manual on joe:** #0046's check 3, now with an expected result: the milestone appears at once, the job ends with the "disc was removed" headline, and the reliability line says `discRemoved`.
