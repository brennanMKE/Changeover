# 0231 — `library <tmdb>` pairs extras with the wrong disc titles when durations are close

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | open |
| **Phase** | 4 — Agentic control |
| **Module** | ChangeoverBridge / LibraryStatus / LibraryProbe |
| **Platform** | macOS |
| **First seen** | 2026-10-07 |

## Description

`changeoverctl library <tmdb>` matches filed extras to disc titles by duration
with what looks like a loose tolerance and first-match-wins. When two titles
have similar durations it credits the wrong one, and then reports the correct
one as missing.

Observed on `tmdb-268896` (Pacific Rim: Uprising), where all 17 extras had
imported successfully:

| Filed extra | Really from | `library` credits | `library` claims missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jake & Nate Discuss Jake's Return (0:38) | title 30 (0:38) | title 11 (0:32) | title 30 |
| Kaiju Brain from Fury (0:35) | title 33 (0:35) | title 9 (0:32) | title 33 |
| I Am Scrapper (2:43) | title 20 (2:42) | title 8 (2:31) | title 20 |

## Why this matters

This is agent-facing output and it is actively misleading. An agent reading it
concludes that titles 20, 30 and 33 were missed and re-imports them — writing
duplicate files for extras that are already correctly in the library. The
failure is worse than a missing report, because it reads as a confident,
specific instruction to do the wrong thing.

It also breaks the invariant the command exists to provide: that an agent can
ask "what is already here?" and act on the answer.

## Suggested fix

Match on the job's recorded `titleIndex` rather than on duration at all. The
encode knows exactly which title it came from; duration is a reconstruction of
information the pipeline already had and threw away.

If the title index is not available for extras filed before it was recorded,
fall back to a **one-to-one nearest-duration assignment** rather than
first-match-wins, so no title can be credited twice and no match is made when a
closer one exists. Greedy first-match is what produces the above: title 11 is
"close enough" to 0:38 and gets consumed before title 30 is considered.

## Relation

Same shape as the recurring projection bug in this project — the decision is
right at the seam and wrong in the thing that reports it. See the pattern
behind #0203, #0208, #0213 and #0214.
