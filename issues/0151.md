## Resolution notes

> 🟢 Resolved — `labelVerdict` returns three answers instead of two (`agrees` / `disagrees` / `cannotJudge`), with a `labelVetoMinimumLength` of 12 characters below which a label is not allowed to judge at all.

## Description

Silence is not agreement.

`answerFitsLabel` answered a veto question — is there grounds to reject this? — and both "the label agrees" and "the label cannot judge" answered *no*. Collapsing them meant a label with nothing to say was read as a label vouching for the answer.

The failure that named this: `USUALLB` is seven characters and can spell nothing. The model's reading, "THE USUAL SUSPECTS", was correct, and the label backed none of it — but the old code reported agreement.

Choosing the threshold mattered more than the shape. A naive fix would have broken five discs in the captured corpus; measurement put the safe floor at 9 and 12 shipped.
