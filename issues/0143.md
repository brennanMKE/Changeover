## Resolution notes

> 🟢 Resolved — the Confirm step probes `Movies/` for the film's `{tmdb-ID}` tag the moment the step appears, shows what it found, and `StartGate` refuses Start until the user explicitly says "Replace the Existing File". Replacement is never silent: `PlexOrganizer` logs a warning before `replaceItemAt`.

## Description

A disc could be ripped straight over a copy already in the library with no warning, forty minutes of encoding spent on a film Plex already had. Nothing looked at the destination until the move itself.

The fix is a pure tag match (`LibraryMatch`) behind an injectable filesystem probe (`LibraryProbe`), surfaced on the Confirm step as a notice with a "Check again" action, and gated in `StartGate.decide` — checked **last**, after every other blocker, so the caption still names the first real problem. The consent is keyed to the movie *and* the disc, so a disc swap wipes it.
