## Description

The Lady in the Water import (`job-20261008-123159-0D74`) failed with
`encode/discUnreadable`. Everything `report` would say about it is:

> the disc could not be read — this is common and is not an app fault

That sentence is true and useless. It does not say **where** the read failed,
**what** HandBrake said, or **how many** read errors there were, and those are
exactly the facts that separate the two live hypotheses:

- a damaged area on the disc (the user says this disc has failed before), or
- the disc being **mounted mid-encode**, disturbing a raw-device read.

They call for different next steps — clean the disc and retry, versus retry from
the now-mounted path — and an agent cannot choose between them.

## What the watch feed showed

- 19:31:59 encode started from `/dev/rdisk4`. The disc was never mounted,
  because the screen was locked at insertion (#0152's path).
- Progress normal at 72 fps to 66–67 %. Last progress event 19:59:10, 67 %,
  fps falling to 65.6.
- Then nothing for eight minutes, and `job.finished` failed at 20:07:19.
- At that moment the disc snapshot reads `isMounted=true`,
  `mountPath=/Volumes/LADY_IN_THE_WATER`. It was mounted at some point during
  the encode, probably when the screen unlocked.

## Root cause for the missing evidence

The evidence is already captured and simply not carried.
`JobOutcome.failed(stage:reason:logTail:)` holds the HandBrake tail and
`HandBrakeFailureClassifier` already reasons over it — `FailurePresenter` even
renders `evidence(in:for:outputPath:)` into the window. `AgentJobReport` exposes
neither, and the wire payloads carry only `failureStage` and `failureReason` as
strings.

Same shape as #0203, #0208, #0213, #0214: the app knows, the projection does
not say.

## Suggested fix

1. Carry `logTail` (or a bounded tail of it) and the classifier's matched
   evidence line into the job report payload.
2. Record the **position** the job reached — last percent, and the chapter or
   timestamp if HandBrake gave one. "Failed at 67 %" is most of the diagnosis
   on its own, and the feed already had it in the last `job.progress` event.
3. Carry a read-error count when HandBrake reports one.

## Related gaps found in the same session

- **`report --job <id>` is refused** ("--job does not apply to report"), even
  though the `import` result tells the agent to use that job id. Either accept
  it or stop advertising the id.
- **No `disc.mounted` event** was emitted although `isMounted` flipped false →
  true during the job. The feed is diffed from snapshots
  (`BridgeStatusDiff.drafts(from:to:)`), so a field that changes and produces no
  event means the diff does not look at it. That transition is exactly what one
  of the two hypotheses turns on.

## Relation

A mid-encode mount disturbing a raw read would be a real bug in its own right,
not just a diagnostic gap — #0152 holds the disc unmounted deliberately and
nothing should be mounting it mid-job. Worth its own ticket if the evidence,
once exposed, points that way. Do not assume it does: the disc has failed before.

See also #0237 for retrying with a different read strategy.
