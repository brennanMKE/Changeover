## Resolution notes

> 🟢 Resolved — each result row carries a credits line (`George Clooney, Violante Placido · dir. Anton Corbijn`), fetched in the same TMDB request as the runtime. The same cast data also became a corroboration signal for automatic selection.

## Description

Reported verbatim: "the poster is too small to see detail… listing the lead actors and director would make The American with George Clooney the obvious choice."

Two films with the same title and a similar year were indistinguishable in the list: a thumbnail poster and a title, nothing a person could actually decide on.

Beyond the UI, the cast turned out to be the signal that rescues a disc whose label says nothing: `CastCorroboration` looks for two surnames of five or more characters matched as whole words in the disc's own menu text, which is independent evidence that the candidate film is the one on the disc.
