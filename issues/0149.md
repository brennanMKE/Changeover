## Resolution notes

> 🟢 Resolved — `MovieAutoSelect` now carries `TermTrust` (`labelBacked` / `unverified`) and `rank()` is split from `decide()`, so a flawless match on a term nothing corroborates is ranked but never auto-selected.

## Description

The half of the Walter Mitty failure that matching could never have caught.

On 2026-09-24 a disc running 6868s (114m) was identified as "The Caretaker". TMDB really does list a 2026 film called The Caretaker at 114 minutes. Title agreed, runtime agreed, exactly one candidate — the matching worked perfectly on a question nobody had checked, namely whether the search term itself came from anything trustworthy. With automatic ripping on it would have filed Walter Mitty under the wrong film.

The fix separates "is this the best candidate for the term?" from "is the term worth trusting?". An unverified term can still be ranked and shown; it cannot start a rip on its own.
