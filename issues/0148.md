## Resolution notes

> 🟢 Resolved — the archive now also records the scan: every title's runtime and chapter count and what HandBrake claimed was the feature, and it writes a record for a disc that was identified but never ripped.

## Description

The archive collected two of the three things an analysis needs. It kept the menus, the OCR text, the stills and the `(label, chosen film)` pair, and threw away every number — how long each title runs, how many chapters it has, what HandBrake claimed was the feature.

Worse, it wrote nothing at all for a disc that was identified but never ripped. On 2026-09-24 that meant the single most informative event of the evening left no trace: a disc labelled `THESECRETLIFEOFWALTERMITTY` identified as "The Caretaker".
